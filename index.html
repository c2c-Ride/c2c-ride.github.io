Things They Don't Teach You in School

Free courses on the practical stuff school skipped: tax, money, politics, health and nutrition. Starting with South Africa. Your AI assistant asks what you need, downloads only the courses for your country, and tutors you at your own pace.

Under development. Please don't rely on these courses yet. They haven't been reviewed by qualified professionals and may contain mistakes.

Start learning

All you need is an AI chat app, on your phone or computer: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or similar.

  1. Tick what fits you. Skip anything you like.
    Where do you live, work or pay tax?
    What do you want to learn about?
    What's your situation?
    How much do you know already?
    How would you like to learn?
  2. Copy this message: 
    Be my tutor. Please read https://things-they-dont-teach-you.github.io/start.md and follow its instructions.
  3. Paste it into a new chat. The tutor starts your course, asking only what you didn't tick. Each lesson is short and works on a free AI plan.
  4. When you stop, the tutor gives you a progress card. Save it in your notes app. Next time, paste the card into a new chat to carry on where you left off.

If the AI says it can't open the link, switch on web search or browsing in the app's settings.

Prefer the terminal?

With git and an AI command-line tool such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI, your courses and progress are saved as files on your computer:

git clone https://github.com/things-they-dont-teach-you/enrol.git school
cd school
claude
